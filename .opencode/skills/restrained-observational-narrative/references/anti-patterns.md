# Anti-Patterns

Common ways prose drifts away from the target style, and how to correct them.

## 1. Author Briefing
**Symptom:** A paragraph that exists mainly to inform the reader of facts the narrator has no urgent reason to state.  
**Correction:** Convert the facts into something the narrator notices, misinterprets, or only partly registers. Move full explanation later or omit it.

## 2. Immediate Emotional Labelling
**Symptom:** “I felt a surge of anger / grief / confusion.”  
**Correction:** Show the physical or behavioural consequence instead (still hands, interrupted routine, prolonged attention to an object). Let the reader name the feeling if they wish.

## 3. Complete Package Revelation
**Symptom:** A single speech or paragraph that delivers the entire background of an event, institution, or relationship.  
**Correction:** Break the information into at least two or three separate moments, each filtered through the narrator’s current limited understanding. Prefer sideways leakage (overheard fragments, incomplete remarks).

## 4. Over-Purposeful Detail
**Symptom:** Every sensory detail is clearly planted to serve a later plot point.  
**Correction:** Include a few details that simply exist — the weight of a cup, the way dust sits on a surface, the exact quality of a sound, a repeated small action — without signalling their future importance.

## 5. Explanatory Dialogue
**Symptom:** Characters speak in ways that conveniently educate the reader or the protagonist.  
**Correction:** Let them speak incompletely, evasively, or about adjacent matters. Allow silence and unfinished sentences. The narrator may fail to grasp the full implication.

## 6. Premature Closure
**Symptom:** The narrator reaches a clear, settled understanding of an event within the same scene in which it occurs.  
**Correction:** Leave a residue of uncertainty. Allow later scenes to revise or complicate the first interpretation. Misreading can stand for a while.

## 7. Ornamented Prose
**Symptom:** Sentences that prioritise elegance or emotional intensity over clarity of observation.  
**Correction:** Strip back to the precise thing seen, heard, measured, or done. Trust the accumulation of plain statements.

## 8. Consistent Competence of Narrator
**Symptom:** The narrator always notices the “right” details and draws near-correct conclusions.  
**Correction:** Allow partial attention, mild misreading, and later correction. The narrator’s cognitive gap is part of the style and can drive the scene.

## 9. Mocking the Narrator’s Limits
**Symptom:** The prose winks at the reader about how naïve or wrong the narrator is.  
**Correction:** Keep any irony gentle and contextual. The text does not ridicule the observer’s restricted understanding; it treats the gap with seriousness.

## 10. Mystery Inflation（谜团膨胀）
**症状：** 为保持神秘，不断新增异常、秘密、隐藏组织或未知技术，最终让读者觉得"一切都是谜"，世界失去真实生活。  
**修正：** 新异常出现前，先检查它是否——(1) 来自已有矛盾；(2) 加深已有问题；(3) 改变已有理解。若只是制造新的疑问，删除。  
**AScombat 专用：** 世界已有汽相、魔法、两盟政治、矿难、学宫、技术竞争，足够丰富。**不要再加"第五势力""古代文明""隐藏实验室"之类的新谜团。**
